Goal: Predict LC (Loss Cost), HALC (Historically Adjusted Loss Cost), and binary CS (Claim Status) for 13,310 test policyholders using 39,928 training auto insurance records.  |  Evaluation: Out-of-sample MSE for LC & HALC  ·  ROC-AUC for CS.  |  Key finding: Customer behavior signals dominate over demographics across all three targets.
Business Problem & Challenges
⚠️
Adverse Selection — Mispricing causes low-risk clients to defect, leaving an unprofitable high-risk pool.
🎯
Zero Inflation (88.85%) — Gaussian regression invalid; Tweedie loss directly models compound Poisson-Gamma distribution.
📊
Heavy Right Tail — LC ranges 0–35,191; MSE dominated by extremes — mitigated via 1st/99th percentile clipping.
🔒
Class Imbalance (11.2%) — scale_pos_weight rebalancing + ROC-AUC (threshold-free) evaluation for CS.

Key Business Insights
Behavior beats demographics. cancel_ratio, renewal timing, and tenure consistently outrank age, vehicle type, and premium across all three targets.
Renewal boundary effect. Claims cluster near contract renewal — signals strategic claiming or adverse selection at policy endpoints.
Loyalty reduces risk. 10+ year policyholders: ~6.1% claim rate vs. ~18.5% for first-year customers — 3× difference.
Auto-specific model. Tweedie assumptions, vehicle features, and behavioral proxies do not transfer to life insurance lines.

Innovations & Methodology
📐 Direct HALC Modeling
HALC modeled as independent Tweedie response — not LC × X̂.18. Avoids compounded prediction error; X.18 is unavailable at test time.
⚡ Tweedie Power Tuning
Power p ∈ {1.1, 1.2, 1.5, 1.8, 1.9} tuned per model and target. Optimal p varies (LC=1.5, GLM=1.1) — confirms p must be a hyperparameter, not a fixed assumption.
🔗 Cross-Model Consistency
Post-processing rule: CS prob < 0.5 forces LC = HALC = 0. Reduces MSE for the 88.85% zero-claim majority; mirrors actuarial two-part model convention.
🛠️ Behavioral Feature Engineering
cancel_ratio, days_since/to_renewal, policy tenure ranked highest in SHAP across all three models — feature engineering was the single largest performance driver.
Conclusion: Three production-ready models delivered — LightGBM Tweedie for LC (MSE 254K) & HALC (MSE 997K), XGBoost Classifier for CS (AUC 0.857). The dominant driver of insurance risk is customer behavior, not demographics: cancellation history, policy tenure, and renewal timing consistently outrank age, vehicle type, and pricing. Actionable for retention pricing, renewal strategy, and underwriting policy.
